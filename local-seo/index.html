What is local SEO?

It's the work that helps your business show up in Google Maps and the local results when someone nearby searches for what you do: your Google Business Profile, reviews, matching listings, and a website that clearly names your services and towns.

How long does local SEO take to work?

It's a months-long effort, not a switch. Early changes usually show up first as more views of your Google listing; bigger ranking gains build over several months as reviews add up. The monthly report shows you what's moving.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

It helps a lot, since Google uses your site to understand what you do. But we can start with your Google Business Profile and listings, and add a site later.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Local Search is month to month. Cancel any time and you keep your Google profile, listings, reviews, and website.
