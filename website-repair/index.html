What we fix most

Small problems that quietly cost you customers.

These are the issues we see over and over on local business websites. Most take an hour or two to fix, and customers notice every one of them.

  • "Not secure" warnings

    Missing or expired security certificates (SSL), so the padlock shows and browsers stop scaring people away.

  • Phone numbers that don't dial

    Tap-to-call links that work, and the right number everywhere, including old numbers buried on inner pages.

  • Broken contact forms

    Forms that silently fail or send to an inbox nobody checks. We test them end to end.

  • Broken links and old pages

    Dead links, discontinued services, last year's specials, and the "© 2019" footer.

  • Hard to use on a phone

    Text too small, buttons too close together, pages that need pinching and zooming.

  • A messy Google listing

    Page titles that don't name your trade or town, and a Google Business Profile that disagrees with your site.

How it works

We fix it where it lives.

You don't need to move your website or change hosts. For the length of the job, you add us as a user on your site (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy, Shopify, and most others allow this), or share access another way you're comfortable with.

We make the fixes, test everything on real phones, and send you a short list of what changed. When we're done, you remove our access. Nothing is changed that you didn't approve.

Fix or rebuild?

Straight advice, either way.

If your site is on a platform that still gets updates, looks decent on a phone, and you can log in to it, it's usually worth fixing. If it's built on something abandoned, nobody has the login, or every page needs work, a rebuild is often cheaper in the long run.

The free 10-minute review tells you which, in plain English, before you spend anything.
