    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Who owns the website?

    You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you get every login. If you ever leave, the site goes with you.

    I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

    No. Phone photos of real jobs work better than stock pictures. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot, and we can come take them.

    Do I have to give up my Facebook page?

    Keep it. Your site links to it and pulls in your reviews. Facebook is good for regulars; your site is how new customers find you.

    What if I already have a website?

    Often it just needs fixing, not replacing. The free review tells you which, and the Fix-It plan covers most small problems.

    Couldn't I just build one on Wix or with AI?

    You could, and some owners do. Most of the people we work with would rather spend that weekend on a job site. We handle everything from photos to Google setup, test it on real phones, and keep it working after launch.

    Am I locked into a contract?

    No. Builds are a flat price, and monthly plans are month to month. Cancel any time and the site, domain, and content stay yours.

    Do I need a monthly plan?

    No. It's optional. Without one, we hand you the keys and show you how to make small changes yourself. The Care Plan covers hosting and edits; Local Search is for owners who want more calls from Google.
