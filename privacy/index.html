Privacy notice

Short version: we only collect what you send us, we use it to help you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

When you use the free review form on this site, we collect what you type into it: your name, business name, phone number, email address, current website or Facebook page, what you need help with, and any notes. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

We don't use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels on this site, and we don't ask you to create an account.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your request, review your website and Google listing as you asked, send you a quote, and keep a record of our conversation. If you become a client, we use it to do the work and send invoices.

We don't sell your information, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Services that handle your information

We use a few outside services to run this site and our business:

  • Google Forms and Google Sheets receive and store form submissions in our Google account.
  • Gmail handles our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers to load them.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep review requests and client records as long as we need them to work with you and to keep accurate business and tax records. If you never become a client, you can ask us to delete your request at any time.

Your choices

Email six18web@gmail.com to see the information we have about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. We'll respond within a reasonable time, usually a few business days. If you'd rather not hear from us, just tell us and we'll stop.

Children

This site is meant for business owners and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
