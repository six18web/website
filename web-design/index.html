What every site includes

Built for the way your customers actually search.

Most people find a local business on their phone, in a hurry, often standing in a parking lot. Every Six18 site is designed for that moment first.

How much does a small business website cost?

A Starter Site is $950 and a Business Site is $2,800, both flat prices. You get a written quote before any work starts, and there are no surprise invoices.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from the day we get your photos and details. Most of the waiting is usually on photos, so we tell you exactly what to shoot.

Do I have to write the content?

No. We write the copy from a short conversation with you, and you approve every word before launch.

Will my new website show up on Google?

We build every site so Google can read your trade and your towns, and we set up your Google Business Profile. New sites usually take a few weeks to be indexed and a few months to climb in local results. Nobody can honestly guarantee a #1 spot, and we won't.

Can I make changes myself?

Yes. We show you how to update hours, prices, and photos. If you'd rather text us the changes, the Care Plan includes up to an hour of edits a month.

What if I already have a website?

It may only need fixing, not replacing. The free review tells you which. See website fixes.

Not sure which site you need?

Tell us about your business. We'll look at your site and Google listing and reply within one business day with what we found. No pressure, no obligation.
